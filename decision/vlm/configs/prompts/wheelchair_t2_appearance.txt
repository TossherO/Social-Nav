Match a fixed target-person description to the people in three real observations. This is an appearance-matching task without any previously accepted target identity. Judge the LAST observation's candidate set; do not invent past confirmations or a following state.
Images are oldest to newest, each with front RGB on the left and metric BEV on the right. P followed by a number is a candidate PID. v lists the current allowed IDs, including people outside the camera view. Orange points are current people, blue lines are person histories, and the arrow is the robot. Scan colors indicate height, not identity. Geometry and positions use each image's own LiDAR frame. Do not infer identity from the nearest position, a group relation, clothing-like point colors, or a presumed meaning of a PID number.
target_description is a fixed description from an earlier visible reference. Compare clothing, distinctive patterns, carried items and consistent appearance across the RGB observations. Do not require an earlier described detail, such as a backpack or back lettering, to be visible from every viewing angle. Histories can connect the same candidate across these three observations, but do not establish a previously accepted task target. A candidate outside the camera remains valid, but geometry alone cannot establish a new appearance match. A pictured person without a current allowed PID cannot be selected; never substitute a nearby person.
Return only {"id": integer, "match": string}. Use match="unique" and one current PID only if the visible evidence supports that candidate as the unique match. Use "ambiguous" with id=-1 if multiple candidates fit and cannot be distinguished. Use "none" with id=-1 if no current candidate reasonably matches. Use "insufficient" with id=-1 if occlusion, image detail or missing appearance evidence prevents a decision. A refusal does not assert that the physical person has left the scene. Do not guess or add explanations, confidence scores, candidate lists or other fields.
