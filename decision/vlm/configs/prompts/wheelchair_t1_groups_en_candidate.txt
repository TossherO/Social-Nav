Identify social groups around the robot and each group's main activity at the LAST observation.
Three real observations are ordered oldest to newest. Each image has front RGB on the left and metric BEV on the right. P followed by a number is a PID; v lists the current IDs allowed in the answer. Orange points are current people, blue lines are person histories, gray lines and the arrow are ego history. Within a BEV, history has ego-motion compensation and may contain brief predictions. x is forward and y is left. Each image, and each position in o, uses its own observation-local LiDAR frame: raw coordinate changes or image drift alone do not establish person motion. Pale height-colored scans show scene geometry, not social relations; color, box orientation and proximity are not group labels.
Use actions, attention to partners, formation and compensated history together to identify groups of at least two people engaged in a shared activity. Clues can complement one another; they need not all be visible in every RGB frame. Valid people outside the camera view remain normal candidates. Keep separate supported groups and omit unrelated people.
Activities: walking means traveling as companions, supported by shared walking and coordinated formation, allowing brief pauses. Talking means conversation, supported by attention to partners, reciprocal orientation, head turns or responsive gestures; continuous face-to-face posture is not required. Photographing means actually taking a photo of another member, not merely holding a phone. Other means another recognizable shared activity. Being briefly near, moving in the same direction, or standing still alone does not establish a group. Conversations can happen while standing or walking. If several activities are supported for the same group, choose photographing, then talking, then walking, then other. Do not call every group walking.
Fields: dt gives relative seconds, ts gives time provenance, e is the extent in metres, o rows are [pid, three xy positions, three normalized RGB boxes, three pixel heights], d gives same-time pair distances, and null means missing.
Return only a JSON object with optional keys walking, talking, photographing, other. Each value is an array of groups, each group an array of at least two current integer PIDs from v. Use each PID at most once across the entire result. Sort members numerically and groups by minimum PID. Omit unused keys and return {} if no supported group exists. No explanations or extra fields.
