Identify observable social groups at the LAST observation for a navigating robot.
Three images are real observations, oldest to newest. Each has front RGB on the left and metric BEV on the right. P7 means integer PID 7. Only current IDs in v may appear in the answer. Historical-only people are not output candidates. Colors and geometric proximity are NOT group labels.
The BEV covers all directions, including people outside the RGB view. A missing RGB box means missing image evidence, not that a person is absent. Orange points are current positions, blue lines are person history, dark gray lines and the arrow are ego history. The solid histories are tracked estimates and may include brief predictions; they are not measurements at every point. Past geometry has ego motion compensation. Each BEV uses that observation's LiDAR frame: x forward, y left. The pale height-colored background is that observation's single LiDAR scan; its color key shows LiDAR Z, not height above ground. It is measured geometry only, not social evidence; blank regions do not prove free space. Box orientation is not gaze.
Geometry fields: v=current PIDs; dt=actual relative seconds; ts=time-source metadata; e=BEV extent in metres; o=rows [pid, three xy positions, three normalized RGB boxes, three pixel heights]; d=pairwise distances at the three times. Null means missing. Distance filtering does not suggest groups.
Report coherent social units of at least two people. Coordinated compact walking formation and consistent paths across time can support companionship. Proximity, the same direction or overlapping image boxes at different depths alone is insufficient. Do not chain nearby strangers into a group or split a coherent group into arbitrary pairs. Use visible actions, mutual orientation, formation and geometry together.
Relations: walking=walking as companions; talking=conversation supported by reciprocal orientation or communication; photographing=actively taking a photo of another member, not merely holding a phone; other=another clearly supported shared activity. A stationary pair is not automatically talking. Queueing is outside this task. If several activities are supported, prioritize photographing, then talking, then walking, then other.
Return ONLY a JSON object whose optional keys are walking, talking, photographing, other. Each value is an array of separate groups; each group is an array of at least two current integer PIDs. Example SHAPE (not an answer): {"walking":[[pid_a,pid_b],[pid_c,pid_d]]}. Omit unused relations; return {} if no definite group is supported. Omit people who are ungrouped or whose relation cannot be established. A PID appears at most once in the entire answer. Sort members numerically and groups by minimum member. No explanations, confidence, role flags, extra keys or Markdown.
